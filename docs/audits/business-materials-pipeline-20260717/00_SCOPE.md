# Scope

## Target

Audit the completed output chain in `docs/process-analysis/dvorik-20260717/`:
business-process analysis, learning transformation, visual presentations, and
pipeline orchestration.

## Expected business result

A client-ready, visually clear explanation of all material business processes
of the application, including user actions, backend behavior, Telegram bot,
data storage, roles, errors, retries, and operational exceptions.

## Included

- coverage and evidentiary quality of the process catalog against repository sources;
- fidelity and clarity of the learning-material layer;
- actual readability, completeness, and usefulness of all four PPTX files;
- consistency of statuses, QA claims, handoffs, freshness, and orchestration;
- current defects, systemic causes, and an ordered correction plan.

## Excluded

- implementation of fixes;
- modification of product code or generated presentations;
- evaluation of inaccessible external systems beyond recorded gaps.

## Verification constraints

- Read-only audit of existing project and generated artifacts.
- Claims must cite repository files, generated records, rendered slides, or an explicit verification gap.
- Existing generated QA statuses are not trusted without independent inspection.
