# P03 — Staff boundary + employee management

## Outcome

Admins can onboard, role, block, profile and record HR events for employees in production, with Core as access authority and Staff as employment authority, without data loss during cutover.

## In scope

- Staff process smoke and observability.
- Migration, validation, cutover and rollback runbook.
- Core-to-Staff identity status propagation.
- Employee dismissal UX/rule and E2E acceptance.
