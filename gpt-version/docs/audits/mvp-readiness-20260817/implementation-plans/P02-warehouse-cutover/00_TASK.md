# P02 — Warehouse MVP scope + initial stock

## Outcome

Launch a physically credible global FIFO warehouse with catalogue, supply receipt, write-off, adjustment and journal; initial balances are reconciled and operational scope cannot be mistaken for location transfer or full inventory.

## In scope

- Initial database generation and evidence lineage.
- Two unverified opening lots.
- FIFO reconciliation, outbox recovery and restart behavior.
- UI/navigation wording and operational procedure for deferred features.
